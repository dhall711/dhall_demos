# UDX AI Hackathon: Comprehensive AI-Powered Analytics

## 🎢 Welcome to Comcast UDX AI-Powered Analytics!

This comprehensive hackathon teaches technical teams how to leverage Snowflake's cutting-edge AI capabilities to build intelligent systems for theme park operations. Choose your learning track based on your role and use case.

## 🎯 Choose Your Learning Track

### Track A: Natural Language to SQL (5 hours)
**Perfect for**: BI Developers, Data Analysts, Business Intelligence Teams  
**Focus**: Building conversational data access for business users

**What You'll Build**:
- Natural Language to SQL Translation Engine
- Conversational Business Intelligence Assistant  
- Advanced Agentic AI Platform with document integration
- Enterprise-Ready Analytics Solution

**Learning Path**:
1. **Basic Translation** (60 min) - Simple NL2SQL queries
2. **Advanced Features** (90 min) - Complex queries & conversation
3. **Final Challenge** (90 min) - Complete NLP2SQL assistant
4. **Agents & Intelligence** (60 min) - Advanced agentic NLP2SQL

---

### Track B: Data Quality & Monitoring (8 hours)
**Perfect for**: Data Engineers, Data Quality Specialists, Platform Teams  
**Focus**: AI-powered data quality monitoring and anomaly detection

**What You'll Build**:
- Autonomous Data Quality Monitoring System
- AI-Powered Anomaly Detection Engine
- Natural Language Insights Generator
- Complete Quality Management Platform

**Learning Path**:
1. **Quality Fundamentals** (90 min) - Data quality concepts & validation

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- Snowflake account with Cortex AI enabled
- Basic understanding of SQL concepts
- Familiarity with business intelligence needs

### Quick Start
1. **Choose Your Track** based on your role and goals
2. **Complete Shared Foundation** setup (30 minutes)
3. **Follow Track-Specific Labs** at your own pace
4. **Explore Advanced Modules** for deeper expertise

### Setup Process
```bash
# 1. Set up shared foundation (required for both tracks)
cd 00-shared-foundation/setup
# Execute unified_setup.sql in Snowflake

# 2. Load business data (required for both tracks)  
cd ../business-data
# Execute unified_business_data.sql

# 3. Choose your track
cd ../../TRACK-A-NLP2SQL     # For Natural Language to SQL
# OR
cd ../../TRACK-B-DATA-QUALITY # For Data Quality & Monitoring
```
